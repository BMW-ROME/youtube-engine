# Project Constitution — Helping others with my voice

This is the governing contract for youtube-engine. Every agent, tool, and contributor must read and obey it before changing architecture, workflows, or publishing gates.

## Identity and non-negotiables

1. Preserve the value chain: **real problem -> real experiment -> real evidence -> clear conclusion -> useful story -> production -> revenue feedback**. A generic AI content farm pivot is prohibited.
2. Every publishable piece must have an explicit real claim, test, or evidence basis. Claims must be traceable to sources; uncertainty and limitations must remain visible. **No evidence -> no case study.**
3. Automation may accelerate research, analysis, and production; it must never fabricate experiments, observations, measurements, or results. Mock outputs and synthetic fixtures are engineering evidence only, never proof of real-world outcomes.
4. Preserve separate, labeled layers for **observation**, **inference**, **audience prediction**, and **business conclusion**. Predictions are not observations; business conclusions require their own evidence.
5. The user's voice and communication strengths remain a strategic asset. Production choices must support that identity rather than silently replace it with generic automated presentation.
6. Preserve the **Walk With Me Panel** and **Multimodal Evidence Architecture** wherever applicable, including evidence provenance and links between source material, interpretation, and story. Changes must explain applicability and preserve these functions; missing implementation is not permission to discard them.
7. **No useful viewer takeaway -> do not publish**, even when content can be generated. Production completion alone never authorizes publication.
8. Optimize for credibility, production capability, and revenue, including **profit per production hour**. Output volume and vanity metrics cannot substitute for these outcomes. Revenue feedback must inform the next real problem or experiment without weakening evidence standards.

## Enforcement

- Architecture and workflow changes must state their effect on these rules and retain evidence and publication gates. If a conflict cannot be resolved, stop the conflicting change and raise it with the project owner.
- Preserve provenance, lifecycle/recovery contracts, and verification evidence. Report actual test commands and outcomes; never equate committed tests with executed tests or mock success with live production success.
- Exceptions or amendments require an explicit project-owner decision recorded with its rationale in the repository. Agents and tools may not silently relax this constitution for convenience or a new provider.
